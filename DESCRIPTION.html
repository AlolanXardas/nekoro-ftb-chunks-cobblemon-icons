A small client-side mod that shows Pokémon from Cobblemon on the FTB Chunks minimap and large map with their own icons.
Made for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack.

Features

• An icon for every Pokémon, with separate icons for shinies
• Regional and alternate forms, female differences and Cobblemon variants have their own icons
• Cobblemon variants without an icon, like Torterra tree types or dyed Wooloo, get a matching color filter
• An outline that follows the Pokémon’s shape, with its own colors for shiny, legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox Pokémon
• Optional pulsing outline for rare Pokémon
• Caught indicator that shows which species you have already caught
• Filters to hide caught species or players’ Pokémon, or to show only rare ones
• Large map tooltip with level, gender, shiny star and variant
• Adjustable icon size and opacity, separately for the minimap and the large map

All options are in the FTB Chunks settings (large map → settings) under Cobblemon Icons.
Icons can be added or replaced with a resource pack, for example for Pokémon from other mods.

Requires

Cobblemon 1.8+
FTB Chunks
FTB Library

Pokémon icons from PokéAPI.
