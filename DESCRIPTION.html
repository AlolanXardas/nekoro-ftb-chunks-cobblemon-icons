Pokémon icons on the FTB Chunks minimap and large map.
Client only.
Requires Cobblemon and FTB Chunks.

Report bugs and share ideas in the comments or on Discord.

Features

  • An icon for every Pokémon, and for its shiny form.
  • Forms, female differences and variants have their own icons.
  • Icons can be added or replaced with a resource pack.
  • Colored outlines for shiny, legendary and other rare Pokémon.
  • A badge for caught or uncaught species, with a choice of icon and color.
  • Hide caught species or players' Pokémon, or show only rare ones.
  • Level, gender and shiny star in the large map tooltip.
  • Icon size and opacity for each map.

Config

  • In the FTB Chunks settings, under Cobblemon Icons.
