Shows Pokémon from Cobblemon on the FTB Chunks minimap and large map with their own icons.
Made for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack.

Client only. Requires Cobblemon, FTB Chunks and FTB Library.

Work in progress

All my projects are being prepared for a big update of my modpack. I'm changing them step by step so they fit together, and they should be ready by the end of this year.
Many graphics are temporary until the mechanics of all my mods are finished.

Features

Icons

  • An icon for every Pokémon, with separate icons for shinies.
  • Regional and alternate forms, female differences and Cobblemon variants have their own icons.
  • Variants without an icon, like Torterra tree types or dyed Wooloo, get a matching color filter.
  • Icons can be added or replaced with a resource pack, for example for Pokémon from other mods.

Outlines

  • An outline that follows the Pokémon's shape.
  • Its own colors for shiny, legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox Pokémon.
  • An optional pulsing outline for rare Pokémon.

Filters

  • A caught indicator shows which species you have already caught.
  • Hide caught species or players' Pokémon, or show only rare ones.

Other features

  • Large map tooltip: Level, gender, shiny star and variant.
  • Icon size and opacity: Set separately for the minimap and the large map.

More information

Config

All options are in the FTB Chunks settings (large map, settings) under Cobblemon Icons.
